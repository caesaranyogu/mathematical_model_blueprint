# Problem Set 01 — From Reality to Variables

## Objective

Learn to identify quantities before writing equations.

### Problem 1

A farm grows vegetables.

Identify at least 15 measurable variables.

Classify each:

- input
- output
- state
- parameter
- constraint
- noise

### Problem 2

A restaurant has declining profits.

List at least 15 variables that might be relevant.

Do not assume you know the cause.

### Problem 3

A person says:

> "I feel more productive when I sleep more."

Identify:

- outcome
- possible explanatory variables
- possible confounders
- possible measurements

### Problem 4

A company says:

> "Our customers love the brand but aren't buying."

Build a list of measurable variables that could distinguish possible explanations.

### Challenge

Choose a phenomenon from your own life.

Write:

1. What you observe.
2. What you want to know.
3. At least 10 variables.
4. How each could be measured.

Do not write an equation yet.
