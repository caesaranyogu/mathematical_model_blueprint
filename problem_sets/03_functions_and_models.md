# Problem Set 03 — Functions and Models

## 1

Define a function representing revenue as a function of customers and average purchase.

## 2

Define a function representing profit as a function of revenue and cost.

## 3

Suppose advertising increases traffic.

Create:

- a linear model
- a nonlinear model

Explain the assumptions.

## 4

Suppose customer conversion increases with trust but decreases with friction.

Create a qualitative mathematical model.

## 5

Build three different mathematical models for the statement:

> "The more money I spend on advertising, the more customers I get, but eventually additional spending produces smaller gains."

## Challenge

Take one of your own observations and construct three competing models.

For each:

- explain the equation
- list assumptions
- identify parameters
- explain what data would distinguish the models
